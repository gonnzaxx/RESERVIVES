## 2. Data We Collect

When you use this application, we may collect and process the following categories of personal data:

**Identification and access data**
- Full name and institutional email address, obtained through your Microsoft account (Microsoft EntraID / Azure AD).
- Role assigned within the system (student, teacher, head of studies, or administrator).

**Service usage data**
- History of space and service reservations at the institute.
- Balance and transaction history of the internal token system.
- Spaces and services marked as favourites.
- Notification preferences configured in the application.

**Technical data**
- Device token for push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), only if you consent to receiving them.

**User-generated content**
- Text and images attached to incident reports.
- Messages sent to the integrated artificial intelligence assistant.
- Votes cast in surveys published by the institution.

---

## 3. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed for the following purposes:

- **Identity management:** to verify your institutional identity and manage access to the application.
- **Service delivery:** to enable space and service reservations, manage the token system, handle waiting lists, and maintain your activity history.
- **Communications:** to send you notifications about the status of your reservations, institutional announcements, and system alerts.
- **Service improvement:** to analyse the use of institutional resources to optimise their management.
- **Technical support:** to process incident reports you submit.

---

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

The processing of your data is based on the following legal grounds provided by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Performance of an institutional relationship** (Art. 6.1.b GDPR): processing is necessary to provide you with the reservation management services of the institution.
- **Legitimate interest** (Art. 6.1.f GDPR): for the proper functioning of the system, platform security, and service improvement.
- **Consent** (Art. 6.1.a GDPR): for sending push notifications, which you may withdraw at any time from the application settings.

Some of the providers listed above (Microsoft and Google) may process data outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers are covered by adequacy decisions of the European Commission or by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the Commission, which ensure a level of protection equivalent to that required within the European Union.

## 7. Retention Period

Your data will be retained for as long as your institutional account remains active in the system. Once your affiliation with IES Luis Vives ends, your data will be deleted or anonymised within a maximum period of **12 months**, unless a legal obligation requires retention for a longer period.

Messages sent to the artificial intelligence assistant are not stored permanently on our servers and are not linked to your identity beyond the active session.

---

## 8. Your Rights

In accordance with the GDPR and applicable Spanish data protection legislation, you have the right to:

- **Access:** obtain confirmation of whether we process your data and receive a copy.
- **Rectification:** request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure:** request deletion of your data when it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.
- **Restriction of processing:** request that the use of your data be restricted in certain circumstances.
- **Data portability:** receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
- **Objection:** object to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Withdrawal of consent:** revoke at any time the consent given for push notifications, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact:

**secretaria@iesluisvives.org**

We will respond within a maximum of one month from receipt of your request.

If you believe that the processing of your data does not comply with applicable regulations, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the **Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD)** at [www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es).

---

## 9. Minors

This application is intended for users **aged 16 or over**, corresponding to Vocational Training students and teaching staff. In accordance with applicable Spanish law, the processing of data of children under 14 requires the consent of their legal guardians.

If we become aware that a user under the age of 14 has provided their data without such consent, we will delete it immediately.

---

## 10. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, accidental loss or destruction, including:

- Encrypted communications via HTTPS for all connections.
- Authentication delegated to Microsoft EntraID, with no passwords stored on our servers.
- Role-based access control within the application.
- Data stored on the institution's own servers.
